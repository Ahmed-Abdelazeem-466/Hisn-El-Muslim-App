The combined **الطقس والطعام والصيام** section is now two distinct cards:

| New category | Chapters | Topics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **الطقس** | 62–68 (7 chapters) | Wind, thunder, rainfall, rain-seeking, clear skies and the new moon |
| **الطعام والصيام** | 69–77 (9 chapters) | Breaking the fast, meals, guests, fasting etiquette and first fruit |

The original Arabic chapter text and references, all 135 chapters and 320 blocks, chapter ordering, quick-access sections, and local reader state remain unchanged.
